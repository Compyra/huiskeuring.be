Why This Tool Exists

The Power of Knowledge

There's a French saying: "Un homme averti en vaut deux" and a Dutch saying: "Een gewaarschuwd man telt voor twee" - A person who is forewarned is worth two. This perfectly captures why this tool was created.

When buying or inspecting a property, knowledge is your greatest asset. Being informed and prepared can save you from costly mistakes and give you the confidence to make better decisions.

A Tool Born from Helping Others

I created this tool because I genuinely enjoy helping people navigate the complex world of property inspection. Over the years, I've witnessed too many situations where people lost money or faced unexpected problems simply because they didn't know what to look for.

Some arrived at viewings unprepared, others missed critical details, and many didn't realize the importance of checking certain aspects until it was too late. Each time I saw this happen, I thought: "There must be a better way."

What This Tool Does for You

This website serves as your personal inspection companion. It helps you:

  • Stay Organized: Never forget to check important aspects during a viewing
  • Document Everything: Keep track of what's good, what needs attention, and what documents you need
  • Make Informed Decisions: Have all the information at your fingertips when it's time to decide
  • Save Money: Identify potential issues before they become expensive surprises
  • Feel Confident: Walk into viewings knowing exactly what to look for

Protect Yourself with Preparation

Buying or renting a property is one of life's biggest decisions. Unfortunately, not everyone in the market has your best interests at heart. While most sellers and agents are honest, a lack of knowledge can leave you vulnerable to overlooking problems or accepting unfavorable terms.

This tool levels the playing field. It empowers you with a systematic approach to property inspection, ensuring nothing slips through the cracks. Whether you're a first-time buyer or an experienced investor, having a comprehensive checklist makes you a more informed and confident participant in the process.

Free, Private, and Made with Care

This tool is completely free to use and respects your privacy completely. All your data stays on your device - no tracking, no data collection, no hidden agendas. Just a simple tool made to help people like you make better, more informed decisions about property.

I hope it serves you well and helps you find the perfect place while avoiding costly mistakes. Good luck with your property search!

How to Use This tool

1. Enter Property Information

Fill in the property details at the top: address, contact person, date, and time. This information will appear in your final report.

2. Use Category Filters

Click category buttons to focus on specific areas (Kitchen, Bathroom, etc.) or use renovation filters to see potential renovation items.

3. Dual Checkbox System

  • ✓ OK Checkbox (Green): Mark items that pass inspection
  • ⚠ Issue Checkbox (Orange): Mark items needing attention or renovation
  • Request Button (Documents): Track which documents need to be requested

4. Add Notes

Click on any item to expand notes. Add specific observations, measurements, or concerns for each item.

5. Generate Report

Click "Generate Report" to create a comprehensive inspection summary. The report includes property info, OK items, issues found, documents to request, and all your notes.

6. Share URL - Save Multiple Inspections

The "Share URL" button creates a special link that captures your current inspection data. This is incredibly useful for:

  • Quick Backup: Save your current inspection by copying the URL to a document or email
  • Multiple Properties: Inspecting several properties? Save each one with its own URL, then start fresh with "Reset All"
  • Sharing: Send the URL to someone else (partner, family member, advisor) so they can view your inspection
  • Archive: Keep a URL library of all properties you've inspected for easy comparison later

How it works: Click "Share URL", copy the generated link, and paste it anywhere safe. When you open that link, all your inspection data will be restored exactly as it was.

7. Auto-Save Feature

All your data is automatically saved to your browser's local storage. You can close the page and return later - your progress will be preserved.

GDPR Compliance

Data Storage

This application stores all data locally in your browser using HTML5 localStorage. No data is transmitted to any server or third party.

Your Rights

  • Right to Access: All your data is visible on this page
  • Right to Deletion: Use the "Reset All" button to delete all stored data
  • Right to Portability: Use "Copy to Clipboard" to export your data
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit any information at any time

Data Controller

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you are the data controller. Compyra does not collect, process, or have access to any of your inspection data.

Cookies

This application does not use cookies. It only uses localStorage for saving your inspection progress on your device.

Privacy Policy

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. This is a client-side application that runs entirely in your browser. All information you enter stays on your device.

Local Storage Only

The application uses your browser's localStorage to save:

  • Property information you enter
  • Checkbox states (OK/Issue)
  • Notes and observations
  • Theme preferences (dark/light mode)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to anyone else.

No Server Communication

This application does not:

  • Send data to external servers
  • Use analytics or tracking
  • Connect to databases
  • Share information with third parties

Data Deletion

To delete all stored data:

  • Click the "Reset All" button in the header
  • Clear your browser's localStorage
  • Clear your browser's cache and site data

Device-Specific Storage

Data is stored per device and per browser. If you use a different device or browser, you will start with a clean slate.
