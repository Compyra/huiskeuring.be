# OUTREACH — backlink & visibility plan for huiskeuring.be

Goal: earn links from Belgian housing, consumer and community sites so buyers
find the checklist when they need it. The pitch is easy: **free, no account,
no ads, no tracking, three languages, 230 verified checks** — the kind of tool
organisations can link to without endorsement risk.

Rules of engagement (Google spam policy & basic ethics):

- Never pay for links, never join link farms or "guest post networks".
- One personal, short mail per target; no mass blasts, one polite follow-up max.
- Offer value first: a deep link that solves *their* reader's problem.
- Keep a record here so nobody gets mailed twice.
